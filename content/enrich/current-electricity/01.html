Every lesson also has a short breather in the middle — a few seconds to rest your eyes and shoulders before the next idea. Take it: it is part of the lesson, not a break from it.

$I = neAv_d$ — read the letters as one word, n-e-A-v, and read the formula as a picture: number of carriers in each cubic metre, each carrying charge e, crossing an Area at drift speed v. Thicker wire, more carriers, or faster drift: more current.

Look up from the screen at the nearest lamp or tube light and let your eyes rest there for three slow breaths.

In the fraction of a second that light took to come on, the electrons in its wire drifted less than the width of a hair. Hold on to that puzzle — the second half of this lesson solves it.
