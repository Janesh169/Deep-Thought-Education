Then: teams of cells. When should you join batteries end to end, and when side by side? The answer depends on what you are trying to drive.

Cells side by side

Series stacks the emfs (and stacks the internal resistances too). Parallel keeps one emf but shares out the internal resistance. So a big load ($R \gg r$) wants series — it is starved of voltage; a tiny load ($R \ll r$) wants parallel — the cells’ own resistance is what is holding it back.
