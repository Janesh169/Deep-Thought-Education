Walking with the current through a resistor: down by $IR$. Walking through a cell from − to +: up by $\varepsilon$. Everything else is the opposite. Say it once at the start of every loop.

Breathe in for a count of four and out for a count of six, three times. A long out-breath is the quickest way to settle a busy mind after a long calculation.
