$\vec F = q\vec v \times \vec B$ is always at right angles to $\vec v$. So a magnetic field can turn a moving charge as sharply as it likes, but it can never speed it up or slow it down. Whenever a question asks for a change in kinetic energy, the magnetic force contributes exactly zero.
